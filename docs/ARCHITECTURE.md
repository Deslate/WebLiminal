# WebLiminal v2 Engine Architecture

The v2 framework (`v2.html`, `src/engine/`) implements a **runtime-synthesis hybrid WebGPU architecture** alongside the untouched v1 physical Poolrooms renderer (`index.html`, `src/render/`): procedural material bases and multi-bounce GI are baked once on the GPU when a level loads, while real-time frames combine hardware rasterization with infinite-resolution analytic surface optics, per-pixel BVH shadows, full-scene BVH glossy reflections, deterministic world-space Contact GTAO, and physical BVH water with live GPU photon caustics. A lazy parallel offline Ground-Truth Monte Carlo Path Tracer (`reference-pt.wgsl`) shares the exact same BVH, analytic materials, area lights, bloom, and AgX tone mapper so real-time lighting and materials can be calibrated directly against unbiased reference renders.

## 1. Map Compilation (`src/engine/map-builder.js`, `uv-atlas.js`, `bvh.js`)

When a v2 level loads (`levels/v2/level0.js`, `levels/v2/poolrooms-v2.js`), its `build(m)` function emits declarative architectural geometry into a `MapBuilder` (rooms, arches, ring walls, spiral stairs, curved tubes, pill window bays, handrails, flush white-enameled recessed ceiling troffers, skylight portals, and architectural collision solids). `compile()` outputs one universal scene package:

- **Indexed Triangle Mesh** (64 bytes/vertex): `position`, `materialId`, `normal`, `tangent` (with handedness), metric tiling `uv` with symmetric crown-aligned arch/ring setting-out and seamless integer-module cylinders, and non-overlapping packed `uv2`.
- **Automatic Lightmap UV2 Atlas (`uv-atlas.js`)**: Shelf-packs all surface charts into a single square atlas (`768²` on hardware GPUs, `384²` on software fallback) with a 2-texel gutter and texel-center alignment; multi-segment primitives (`cylinder`, `curvedTube`, `trumpetColumn`) each share a single compact UV2 chart.
- **Flat Binned-SAH BVH (`bvh.js`)**: Compiles all scene triangles into 32-byte nodes and 112-byte Möller-Trumbore records carrying interpolated surface `uv` and lightmap `uv2`, allowing any runtime reflection, shadow, refraction, or offline path-tracing ray to evaluate analytic materials and baked multi-bounce GI in O(1).
- **Analytical Light & Skylight List**: Up to 64 analytical area light quads (recessed fluorescent troffers, frosted backlit windows, glowing signs, skylight portals) with precomputed power CDFs.
- **Collision Structure (`collision.js`)**: Walkable room bounds, 2D solid boxes/circles (including exact circular columns, lower spiral stair steps, curved tubes, and Pitfalls shaft guards), and elevation zones (ramps, stairs, raised decks).

## 2. Startup GPU Synthesis (`bake-textures.wgsl`, `mipmap.wgsl`)

Only materials referenced by the active level are baked:

1. `bake-textures.wgsl` synthesizes `512×512` (or `256×256` on software fallback) seamless periodic textures into three `rgba8unorm` `texture_2d_array` resources (`albedoRoughTex`, `normalHeightTex`, `metaTex`).
2. `mipmap.wgsl` builds the complete mipmap chain using **Toksvig normal-variance folding** to suppress distant specular aliasing.
3. For both Poolrooms porcelain (`evalAnalyticCeramicFull`) and Level 0 architectural interiors (`evalAnalyticInterior` in `bvh-common.wgsl`), fine geometric detail is evaluated **analytically at infinite resolution** with smooth `fwUV` Nyquist anti-aliasing (zero shimmer while panning):
   - **Poolrooms porcelain**: primary-ray 3D height-field relief intersection (`ceramicReliefUV`, `3.4 mm` recessed mortar bed + `±0.8 mm` hand-laid tile lippage), outward convex `5.2–6.8 mm` rounded ceramic bevel shoulders with per-axis anisotropic Nyquist band-limiting, `±1.4°` hand-laid tile mounting tilt, subtle kiln glaze curvature & waviness, raking-light groove self-occlusion, broad satin indirect sheen, and 4-tap footprint slope-moment roughness broadening.
   - **Level 0 interiors**: four seed-selected vintage wallpaper variants (fine 2002 chevron-arrow damask, vertical micro-dot columns, fine double pinstripes, and crisp Baroque ogee floral damask with 0.60 m roll butt-seams, ink-vs-paper satin contrast, and subtle moisture aging), seamless multi-scale fibrous broadloom carpet pile, painted commercial drywall stipple, flush `1.20×0.60 m` baked-enamel drop-ceiling T-bar grids with recessed tile bevels and fissured acoustic mineral pinholes, and twin-tube prismatic acrylic troffer diffusers.

## 3. Startup & Progressive GI Baker (`bake-gbuffer.wgsl`, `bake-lightmap.wgsl`, `denoise-lightmap.wgsl`)

1. **UV2 G-Buffer Rasterization**: Renders the mesh in UV2 space for exact world positions and geometric normals (`rgba32float` / `rgba16float`) with 2-texel gutter dilation.
2. **Exact Local-Importance Area-Light & 3-Bounce GI Lightmap**:
   - **Sunlit levels (Poolrooms)**: Direct sunlight is excluded from primary lightmap texels and evaluated per-pixel via world-locked BVH soft shadows at runtime, while primary texels accumulate smooth skylight/window fill, dominant incoming direction, and short-range baked AO.
   - **Multi-room fluorescent interiors (Level 0, `perPixelAreaLights: false`)**: Primary lightmap texels integrate all 43 rectangular `1.20×0.60 m` fluorescent troffers using exact local-importance discrete CDF resampling (`w_i = power_i * cosSurf * cosLight / max(dist2, 0.14)`, zero wasted cross-room rays, zero Top-K truncation, and zero hard point-light shadows) combined with unbiased 3-bounce multi-bounce GI (`bounceBoost = 1.00`) and tight localized crevice AO (`aoRadius = 0.52 m`).
   - **Physical Water GI Filtering**: Indirect bounces traversing underwater paths attenuate by exact Beer-Lambert absorption so sunlit pool floors bounce luminous emerald-turquoise light onto ceilings.
3. **Edge-Preserving Bilateral Denoise & Localized AO**: A 4-pass geometry-aware À-trous filter (`step = 1, 2, 4, 8`) adapts automatically to the level's lighting mode — tight luminance/plane edge-stopping with localized fine-pass AO (`step <= 2`, `dist2 < 0.14`) on baked area-light interiors (Level 0), and wide planar/curved indirect-GI smoothing (`normMin = 0.84`, `maxDist2 = 2.8`) on sunlit levels (Poolrooms) so indirect bounce and dark tunnels remain velvety smooth with zero mottle.

## 4. Real-Time Frame Graph (`renderer.js`)

Per frame, the renderer executes six passes (internal resolution capped at `<= 1920×1080` on high-DPI displays):

1. **Sky Backdrop + Opaque PBR + Analytic Materials + MRP Area Specular (`gbuffer-pbr.wgsl`)**: Evaluates `evalAnalyticCeramicFull` / `evalAnalyticInterior`, directional lightmap shading, 1-ray world-locked sun-center visibility on sunlit levels, and **Most Representative Point (MRP)** rectangular area-light GGX specular highlights so enameled ceiling T-bars, baseboards, wall outlets, and satin wallpaper ink catch crisp elongated fluorescent highlights.
2. **Half-Res Deterministic BVH Reflections + Separable Roughness Cone Filter + Edge-Only Soft Sun Penumbra + Contact GTAO (`ssr.wgsl`)**: `traceSSR` traces 1 deterministic specular BVH ray per half-res quad (zero IGN boiling) with GGX grazing-roughness attenuation and per-tile mounting micro-tilt (`±0.9°`) smoothed by separable roughness-adaptive bilateral passes (`filterSSRH`, `filterSSRV`). Full-res `compositeSSR` resolves fixed 12-tap Vogel solar disk penumbras strictly on sun-shadow boundaries, upsamples filtered reflections masked strictly by full-res `ar.a` (so matte grout joints stop reflections cleanly), and applies a deterministic zero-noise **6-direction × 3-step world-space Contact GTAO** (`0.42 m` radius).
3. **Optional Two-Solver Physical Water & Live GPU Photon Caustics (`water.js`, `water-finite-depth.*`, `water-body-waves.*`, `wake-trail.js`, `water-reconstruct.wgsl`, `water-caustics.wgsl`, `water-render.wgsl`)**: Zero cost on dry levels (Level 0). On Poolrooms, runs a player-tracking `20.48×20.48 m` (`17.92 m` software) lively Chebyshev finite-depth Neumann background solver plus a `256×256` spectral FFT wading wake solver, refracting live chromatic sunlight caustics and rendering BVH Snell refractions with exact v1 Beer-Lambert aquamarine optics (`[0.34, 0.075, 0.040]`, `0.44 m` shallow depth) and $(n_1/n_2)^2 = 1/1.333^2$ radiance conservation.
4. **BVH-Occluded Volumetric Atmosphere (`volumetric.wgsl`)**: 12-step half-res deterministic raymarch for sunlight shafts and soft area-light haze, smoothed by a 5×5 Gaussian filter.
5. **HDR Bloom Halation (`postprocess.wgsl`)**: Half-res 2×2 box-prefiltered bright-pass extraction (`threshold = 2.35`) and continuous unit-stride 17-tap separable Gaussian blur giving overhead troffers and skylights soft optical halation.
6. **Composite & Filmic Present (`postprocess.wgsl`)**: Exponential extinction fog, volumetric mist, optical bloom, v1-matched **ACES Filmic** with subtle radial lens chromatic aberration & luminance-weighted emulsion grain on Poolrooms (`hasWater`), Troy Sobotka **AgX Filmic** on dry interiors (Level 0), vignette, and optional Found-Footage camcorder mode (`V`).

## 5. Lazy Parallel Offline Ground-Truth Path Tracer (`reference-pt.wgsl`)

Exposed via `window.__WEBLIMINAL__.renderReferencePT({ spp, batchSpp })` and allocated lazily (zero memory and zero frame cost during normal gameplay). Traces unbiased 5-bounce Monte Carlo paths with local-importance NEE over all scene area lights and direct sun, physical dielectric water Fresnel reflection & Snell refraction with Beer-Lambert absorption, Fresnel-split GGX + Lambertian BSDF, and shared `evalAnalyticInterior` / `ceramicReliefUV` / `evalAnalyticCeramicFull`, then feeds the resolved HDR image through the identical bloom, depth fog, and `present` pass for apples-to-apples real-time quality calibration across both dry interiors and flooded Poolrooms chambers.

## 6. Motion-Driven Physical Procedural Audio & Classic Backrooms BGM (`src/engine/audio.js`, `src/engine/bgm.js`)

Zero external audio files and zero continuous movement-volume noise loops. Movement strictly drives collision-resolved footsteps (`wakeTrail` in water with a `0.82 m` heavy knee-deep stride so cadence scales naturally with movement speed — `~0.96 s/step` at `0.85 m/s` vs `~0.57 s/step` when Shift-wading — and `0.86 m` stride-distance tracking on dry floors, synced 1:1 with a speed-damped footstep head-bob dip), and footsteps strictly drive sound:

- **Hydrodynamic & Dry Foley Banks (`src/engine/audio.js`)**:
  - **Wading bank (`6 × 0.82 s`)**: Two-phase noise-excited stochastic liquid-formant synthesis with speed-scaled splash loudness and pitch — Phase A smooth submerged shin-drag displacement (`100–490 Hz`, organic random-walk modulation with zero periodic LFO), Phase B distinct surface-break plunge splash driven by parallel drifting resonant fluid-cavity bandpass streams (`Q = 4.2–5.0`, `250–2900 Hz`) with irregular Poisson water-clump envelopes, sparse wet water-sheet slaps, subtle buried entrainment bubbles, and muffled submerged heel plant, followed by Phase C receding wake wash and delicate runoff droplets routed generously into the 3.0 s glazed-tile natatorium hall reverb (`reverbSend = 0.72`).
  - **Dry-surface banks**: Muffled commercial broadloom carpet (`6 × 0.22 s`) and wet porcelain tile (`6 × 0.18 s`), plus gentle natatorium stillness wall-laps and sparse isolated ceiling drips.
- **Iconic Level 0 Harsh Fluorescent Ballast Ambience (Default on Level 0)**:
  - Dual slightly-detuned `120 Hz` rectified magnetic ballast sawtooth cores (`119.78 Hz` / `120.24 Hz` spatial fixture beating), `60 Hz` AC mains fundamental, `240.35 Hz` metallic troffer chassis buzz, faint constant `3120 Hz` tube filament whine, resonant `360 Hz` & `960 Hz` peaking EQ, and irregular stereo-panned overhead flicker/arc stutter bursts.
- **Classic Backrooms Pure-Code BGM (`src/engine/bgm.js`)**: Toggleable anytime via `N`, `#btn-bgm`, or the Inspector (with live volume slider), with smart per-level defaults:
  - **Poolrooms (`poolrooms`, defaults ON)**: ***Hi* — TEMPOREX** (`76 BPM`, 4/4, pure-code Dexed/DX7 multi-operator FM electric piano & warm lo-fi organ pad over the iconic `Fmaj7 → Dmaj7 → Dm7 → Am7 → D7` progression with tape wow/flutter and natatorium echo).
  - **Level 0 (`backrooms`, defaults OFF — Ambient Fluorescent Hum)**: ***It's Just a Burning Memory* (*Heartaches*, 1931)** (`79 BPM` Ab Major muted ballroom horn, foxtrot stride piano, 78 rpm shellac crackle, and distant hall reverb when toggled ON).
