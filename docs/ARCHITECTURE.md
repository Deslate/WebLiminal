# WebLiminal v2 Engine Architecture

The v2 framework (`v2.html`, `src/engine/`) implements a **runtime-synthesis hybrid WebGPU architecture** alongside the untouched v1 physical Poolrooms renderer (`index.html`, `src/render/`): procedural material bases and multi-bounce indirect GI are baked once on the GPU when a level loads, while real-time frames combine hardware rasterization with analytic ceramic optics, per-pixel BVH shadows, full-scene BVH glossy reflections, and physical BVH water with live GPU photon caustics.

## 1. Map Compilation (`src/engine/map-builder.js`, `uv-atlas.js`, `bvh.js`)

When a v2 level loads, its `build(m)` function emits declarative architectural geometry into a `MapBuilder` (rooms, arches, ring walls, spiral stairs, curved tubes, pill window bays, handrails, flared truncated-pyramid capitals `flaredCapital`, smooth concave hyperbolic-flare mushroom columns `trumpetColumn`, recessed/surface ceiling fixtures, pillar/wall-mounted `verticalTubeLight` fixtures, and crossed swaying botanical `foliageVine` ivy ribbons). `compile()` outputs one universal scene package:

- **Indexed Triangle Mesh** (64 bytes/vertex): `position`, `materialId`, `normal`, `tangent` (with handedness), metric tiling `uv` with symmetric crown-aligned arch/ring setting-out and seamless integer-module cylinders, and non-overlapping packed `uv2`.
- **Automatic Lightmap UV2 Atlas (`uv-atlas.js`)**: Shelf-packs all surface charts into a single square atlas (`768²` on hardware GPUs, `384²` on software fallback) with a 2-texel gutter and texel-center alignment; multi-segment primitives (`cylinder`, `curvedTube`, `trumpetColumn`, `foliageVine`) each share a single compact UV2 chart.
- **Flat Binned-SAH BVH (`bvh.js`)**: Compiles all scene triangles into 32-byte nodes and 112-byte Möller-Trumbore records carrying interpolated surface `uv` and lightmap `uv2`, allowing any runtime reflection, shadow, or refraction ray to evaluate analytic materials, botanical vine alpha-cutouts, and baked multi-bounce GI in O(1).
- **Analytical Light & Skylight List**: Up to 64 analytical area light quads (recessed troffers, high-bay boxes, vertical column/wall fluorescent tubes, frosted backlit windows, glowing signs, skylight portals) with precomputed power CDFs.
- **Collision Structure (`collision.js`)**: Walkable room bounds, 2D solid boxes/circles (including exact circular mushroom columns, lower spiral stair steps, curved tubes, and pit-edge guards), and elevation zones (ramps, stairs, raised decks).

## 2. Startup GPU Synthesis (`bake-textures.wgsl`, `mipmap.wgsl`)

Only materials referenced by the active level are baked:

1. `bake-textures.wgsl` synthesizes `512×512` (or `256×256` on software fallback) seamless periodic textures into three `rgba8unorm` `texture_2d_array` resources (`albedoRoughTex`, `normalHeightTex`, `metaTex`). For ceramic porcelain presets, organic glaze base tone is baked here while flat architectural tile faces, millimeter mortar joints, soft 3.2–4.6 mm bevelled ceramic lips, kiln-fired tonal variation, glaze waviness, and weathering are evaluated analytically at infinite resolution in the shading passes. Level 0 supports four seed-selected vintage wallpaper variants (fine 12 cm chevron damask, vertical micro-dot columns, vertical pinstripes, and Baroque floral damask), wool-loop commercial broadloom carpet, and bright ivory acoustic tiles; Level 1 synthesizes raw/painted concrete, vertical galvanized `corrugated_metal` (preset 19), swirling emerald `moss_floor` (preset 20), backlit botanical `foliage_vine` (preset 21), and seed-selected concrete floor regimes (Aquila mirror-wet tire-track puddles, High-Bay two-tone poured concrete, Vaulted Garage white parking bay lines).
2. `mipmap.wgsl` builds the complete mipmap chain using **Toksvig normal-variance folding** to suppress distant specular aliasing.

## 3. Startup & Progressive Indirect GI Baker (`bake-gbuffer.wgsl`, `bake-lightmap.wgsl`, `denoise-lightmap.wgsl`)

1. **UV2 G-Buffer Rasterization**: Renders the mesh in UV2 space for exact world positions and geometric normals (`rgba32float` / `rgba16float`) with 2-texel gutter dilation.
2. **Split Direct/Indirect Lightmap**:
   - **Sunlit levels (Poolrooms)**: Direct sunlight is excluded from primary lightmap texels and evaluated per-pixel via world-locked BVH soft shadows at runtime, while primary texels accumulate smooth skylight/window fill, dominant incoming direction, and short-range baked AO.
   - **Per-pixel indoor & hybrid levels (Level 0, Level 1)**: Setting `atmosphere.perPixelAreaLights` (or omitting `sun`) excludes primary direct area lights from primary lightmap texels and evaluates them at full pixel resolution in `gbuffer-pbr.wgsl`, allowing hybrid sunlit + underground levels (such as Level 1's enclosed Aquila/Warehouse/Mushroom sectors beside an open-sky Overgrown Courtyard) to combine razor-sharp sunbeams and per-pixel fluorescent fixtures with smooth 3-bounce indirect GI.
   - **3-Bounce Indirect GI with Physical Water Filtering & Botanical Shadows**: All indirect bounces sample all sun and area lights, attenuate underwater paths by exact Beer-Lambert water absorption, and pass shadow rays cleanly through alpha-cutout `foliage_vine` leaf gaps.
3. **Plane-Aware Bilateral Denoise & Gutter Dilation**: A 4-pass plane-coplanar geometry-aware À-trous filter (`step = 1, 2, 4, 8`) eliminates low-frequency bounce mottling without bleeding across stairs or corners, followed by gutter dilation into `rgba16float` textures.

## 4. Real-Time Frame Graph (`renderer.js`)

Per frame, the renderer executes six passes (internal resolution capped at `<= 1920×1080` on high-DPI displays):

1. **Sky Backdrop + Opaque PBR + Analytic Architectural Materials + Per-Pixel Direct Shadows (`gbuffer-pbr.wgsl`)**: Hardware-rasterized PBR with v1-calibrated warm travertine-ivory glazed porcelain (`evalAnalyticCeramicFull`: genuinely flat fired faces, 3.2–4.6 mm rounded ceramic bevel lip with gentle bevel-shoulder shading, box-filtered natural cement mortar, Nyquist-attenuated micro-glaze slopes, and 4-tap footprint slope-moment GGX roughness broadening), commercial architectural grids (`1.20×0.60 m` drop-ceiling T-bars, `0.60 m` wallpaper roll seams, `0.80 m` concrete lift joints, `2.40 m` floor saw-cut joints), vertical galvanized corrugated ribs, swirling moss contours, and procedural alpha-cutout botanical ivy vines (`evalFoliageVine` stem + 3-lobed veined leaves with chlorophyll backlit translucency and dappled BVH shadow pass-through). Sunlit levels trace a 1-ray world-locked sun-center visibility ray written to `sunDirect`; levels with `perPixelAreaLights` rank up to 64 facing rectangular area lights by unshadowed energy (zero rays) and evaluate top-6 fixtures per pixel with adaptive 2-to-8 stratified interior quad soft shadows on penumbra edges (zero screen-space noise or lightmap blur).
2. **Half-Res Deterministic BVH Reflections + Separable Roughness Cone Filter + Edge-Only Soft Sun Penumbra (`ssr.wgsl`)**: `traceSSR` selects the glossiest non-grout pixel per 2×2 quad and traces 1 deterministic specular BVH ray (zero screen-space IGN boiling), shading reflected hits with baked GI plus direct sun and/or top-3 indoor area lights (so Aquila Sector's wet tire-track puddles mirror blinding vertical column/wall fluorescent tubes). Separable horizontal/vertical bilateral passes (`filterSSRH`, `filterSSRV`) project the GGX angular lobe radius into screen space so wet puddles remain mirror-sharp while glazed porcelain reflects smoothly. Full-res `compositeSSR` resolves a fixed 12-tap deterministic Vogel solar disk exclusively on detected sun-shadow boundaries (zero screen-space crawling on camera motion) and upsamples filtered reflections with sub-texel bilinear bilateral weights.
3. **Optional Two-Solver Physical Water & Live GPU Photon Caustics (`water.js`, `water-finite-depth.*`, `water-body-waves.*`, `wake-trail.js`, `water-reconstruct.wgsl`, `water-caustics.wgsl`, `water-render.wgsl`)**: Zero cost on dry levels. On flooded levels, a **player-tracking local simulation window** (`20.48×20.48 m` at `8 cm` cells on desktop GPU, shifting seamlessly without edge-reset artifacts as the player explores):
   - **Background Solver (`water-finite-depth`)**: Solves the linear finite-depth gravity-capillary equation with exact Chebyshev polynomial $G(L) = \sqrt{L}\tanh(H\sqrt{L})$ over the 9-point Neumann wet-domain graph Laplacian (respecting walls, columns, stairs, and raised pool decks), driven by band-limited stochastic pressure forcing for fine organic multi-scale pool ripples.
   - **Wading Wake Solver (`water-body-waves` + `wake-trail.js`)**: A `256×256` GPU 2D spectral FFT initial-value solver integrating $\omega^2(k) = (9.81 k + 0.000073 k^3)\tanh(k H)$ driven by smoothed moving body displacement (emerging bow crest and diverging Kelvin V-wake arms), collision-resolved alternating left/right footstep pressure pulses, and ballistic kick-splash impacts.
   - **Shared Composition & Live Photon Caustics (`water-reconstruct.wgsl`, `water-caustics.wgsl`)**: Composes both physical fields with radius-2 Neumann ghost-cell extrapolation across dry boundaries ($\partial h/\partial n = 0$) so Catmull-Rom bicubic slopes stay smooth right up to walls; refracts live sunlight photons through the surface with RGB chromatic dispersion (IOR 1.325 / 1.333 / 1.342) into an atomic world-space caustic irradiance grid that deforms live with every wading step.
   - **Ray-Heightfield BVH Water Surface (`water-render.wgsl`)**: Renders a raised wave-crest bounding envelope with hardware Early-Z culling (`depthCompare: "less"`), 4-step damped Newton ray-heightfield intersection against `sceneDepthTex` for an organically undulating water-wall contact line, subtle capillary meniscus curl, camera-facing normal clamping, wet-side BVH Snell refraction of submerged analytic tiles illuminated by live caustics with exact RGB Beer-Lambert absorption and Snell solid-angle radiance conservation $(n_1/n_2)^2 = 1/1.333^2$, and full-scene BVH mirror reflections.
4. **BVH-Occluded Volumetric Atmosphere (`volumetric.wgsl`)**: 12-step half-res raymarch with deterministic 2×2 ordered step phase, BVH-occluded sunlight shafts (Henyey-Greenstein phase), and deterministic top-2 strongest in-range skylight/area-light mist scattering (scanning up to 64 lights) smoothed by a 5×5 Gaussian filter.
5. **HDR Bloom Halation (`postprocess.wgsl`)**: Half-res 2×2 box-prefiltered bright-pass extraction (`threshold = 2.35`) and continuous unit-stride 17-tap separable Gaussian blur preventing comb-striping on thin industrial fluorescent tubes while haloing emissive fixtures and lightwells.
6. **Composite & AgX Present (`postprocess.wgsl`)**: Exponential extinction fog, volumetric mist, optical bloom halation, Troy Sobotka **AgX Filmic Tone Mapping** with v1-matched shadow-toe depth, vignette, clean static 8-bit de-banding dither (zero animated grain in clean mode), and optional Found-Footage camcorder mode.

## 5. Motion-Driven Physical Procedural Audio & Liminal BGM (`src/engine/audio.js`, `src/engine/bgm.js`)

Zero external audio files and zero continuous movement-volume noise loops. Movement strictly drives collision-resolved footsteps (`wakeTrail` in water and stride-distance tracking on dry floors, synced 1:1 with a smooth damped footstep head-bob dip), and footsteps strictly drive sound:

- **Startup Physical Minnaert-Bubble Foley Banks**: Pre-synthesizes multi-variation stereo `AudioBuffer` banks at startup using Leighton (1994) / van den Doel (2005) Minnaert bubble physics ($f_0 = 3.26 / R$, rising-pitch chirp, $\delta = 0.043 f_0 + 0.0014 f_0^{3/2}$, power-law bubble size spectrum, wet droplet micro-clicks, and hydrodynamic cavity pulses):
  - **Wading bank (`6 × 0.68 s`)**: 4-phase stride (rear foot lift cavity bubbles -> 92+ Minnaert bubbles shin sweep & splash -> submerged floor plant -> 6–9 individual calf drips in the inter-step gap) with dry-forward clarity and alternating L/R panning.
  - **Dry-surface banks**: Damp commercial broadloom carpet (`6 × 0.24 s`), gritty/wet-puddle concrete (`6 × 0.34 s`), and wet porcelain tile (`6 × 0.20 s`).
  - **Ambient stillness**: Frequency-damped stereo convolution reverb (3.1 s IR), profile-tuned room air & 60/120 Hz ballast or 46 Hz ventilation hum, plus sparse echoing ceiling/pipe Minnaert droplets and gentle wall-lap bubble glugs.
- **Pure-Code Procedural Liminal BGM (`src/engine/bgm.js`)**: Lookahead Web Audio clock scheduler with dedicated tape saturation, stereo ping-pong tape echo, 4.2 s dark hall convolution reverb, organic analog tape wow/flutter, and subtle shellac vinyl crackle bed — toggleable independently from SFX via key `N`, the top HUD button, or the Inspector panel (with live volume slider):
  - **Level 0 (`backrooms`) — *Ballroom Tape Loop (1934)***: 72 BPM 3/4 haunted ballroom waltz in Eb major / C minor (`Abm6` minor-iv & `dom7b9` nostalgic turns) with muted 1930s horn lead, brushed vintage Rhodes accompaniment, hollow string pads, gramophone bandpass EQ, and vinyl crackle.
  - **Poolrooms (`poolrooms`) — *Submerged Sunbeam***: 62 BPM 4/4 floating Db Maj9/Lydian dreamcore ambient with DX7 glassy FM electric piano arpeggios, sparse crystalline water-bells, tape-warbled Juno pads, and velvet sub-bass.
  - **Level 1 (`parking`) — *Concrete Echoes***: 68 BPM 4/4 D Dorian / A minor9 VHS analog tape ambient with detuned cassette pads, echoing metallic kalimba/Rhodes ostinato, mournful analog lead, and soft subterranean pulse.
